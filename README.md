## About

**SVEvents** is a personal web platform designed to streamline event organization — bringing guest check-ins, sales tracking, schedules, live displays, and shift planning together in one place.

While the platform is built and maintained primarily for my own private use, the codebase is public in the spirit of transparency. Feel free to look around, and if you notice anything that could be improved or optimized, I'm always happy to hear your thoughts.
